# University Modules (2025/2026 - 2027/2028)

Rolling reference for the owner's undergraduate Computer Science degree at
**University of Reading** (Department of Computer Science). It summarises,
per academic year: dates, the modules taken, the instructions/objectives from
the departmental briefings, and each module's official catalogue entry on the
university module catalogue.

- Full week-by-week study plans for Year 2: `YEAR2_SEMESTER1.md`,
  `YEAR2_SEMESTER2.md`.
- Local briefing copies: `university_courseworks/year1`, `year2`, `year3`.
- Catalogue links resolve for the owner's institutional login; school code is
  `MPS CS|MPS MATHST|MPS MET` throughout.

## Degree-Wide Rules (from the briefings)

- **Progression (to Year 2):** at least 40% in individual modules totalling
  no less than 80 credits, and at least 30% across the 120 credits. Fails can
  be resat in summer, but a resit mark is capped at 40%.
- **Degree classification:** weighted 1/3 (Year 2 average) + 2/3 (final year
  average); the final-year Individual Project (CS3IP) carries double weight.
- **Discipline:** submit all coursework on time; start project work early.

## Year 2 - 2026/2027

**Dates:** Semester 1 Mon 28 Sep 2026 - Thu 17 Dec 2026 (week off Mon 2 Nov -
Sun 8 Nov); Semester 2 Mon 1 Feb 2027 - Fri 28 May 2027 (assessment period
Mon 10 May - Fri 28 May).

### Modules (2026/27)

| Semester | Module | Title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CS2DA | Data Structures and Algorithms |
| 1 | CS2PP | Programming in Python |
| 1 | CS2SE | Software Engineering & Professional Development |
| 2 | CS2AI | Artificial Intelligence |
| 2 | CS2ON | Operating Systems and Computer Networking |
| 2 | CS2SD | Software Systems Design |

### Instructions & objectives

- All modules are compulsory. CS2AI and CS2SD include groupwork elements.
- CS2DA: 50% open exam + 50% technical report (7 pages, due Week 11).
  Topics: trees/heaps/graphs, divide and conquer, greedy, dynamic programming.
- CS2PP: 40% set exercises (due Week 7) + 60% group application project
  (demonstrated in January assessment week).
- CS2SE: group technical report Stage 1 (50%) and Stage 2 (50%) - architecture,
  requirements, iterative development, testing, risk and ethics.
- CS2ON: 50% technical report (7 pages, due Teaching Week 10) + 50% written
  exam (2 hours) - OS, memory, storage/IO, networking, security.
- CS2SD: design report Stage 1 (group, 40%, due Week 7) + Stage 2 (individual,
  60%, due Week 12) - OOAD, UML, domain modelling, validation, evaluation.
- Full week-by-week plans and readiness gaps: `YEAR2_SEMESTER1.md` /
  `YEAR2_SEMESTER2.md`.

  CS3IV and CS3TM are the closest matches (confirmed list may change at
